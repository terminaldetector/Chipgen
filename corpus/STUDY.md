36% of moves repeat the note, 22% are a step of a tone or less, and 23% leap a fifth or more — mostly octaves. There is very little in between. A line that leaps constantly will not sound like this music, and neither will one that never repeats a note.

## Tempo, velocity, vibrato

- **Tempo**: 72 to 195 BPM, median 106.
- **FM velocity**: median 25 of 127, with the middle 80% between 7 and 64. Notes are not all struck at full level.
- **Vibrato**: 2216 spans. Depth median 24 cents (middle 80%: 12-88), speed median 5.7 Hz. That is well under a semitone — vibrato here colours a note, it does not bend it.
