# What Mega Drive music does, measured

From 90 transcribed tracks, 94594 notes. Every number below is counted from the corpus, not asserted.

## Channel roles

Median register and how often each channel is used at all. The roles are not a convention someone chose — they fall out of the chip: FM0 has the lowest median register of the six, and FM5 is missing from most because the DAC takes channel 6 for drums.

| voice | tracks | p10 | median | p90 | typical role |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| fm0 | 99% | G-1 | A-2 | B-4 | harmony / bass |
| fm5 | 43% | F-1 | A-2 | G#-4 | harmony |
| fm1 | 100% | D-1 | B-2 | G-5 | harmony / bass |
| fm3 | 99% | A-0 | D-3 | F#-5 | harmony |
| fm2 | 100% | F-1 | D#-3 | B-6 | harmony / bass |
| fm4 | 98% | G#-1 | D-4 | A#-6 | harmony |
| psg2 | 11% | D-3 | G-4 | G#-6 | harmony / lead |
| psg1 | 56% | F-3 | A-4 | B-5 | harmony |
| psg0 | 57% | B-3 | D-5 | E-6 | harmony / lead |

## How melodies move

Interval from one note to the next on the same voice, in semitones:

```
  -12   3.6%  ###
   -5   3.5%  ###
   -3   3.1%  ##
   -2   4.8%  ####
   -1   5.5%  ####
   +0  40.5%  ##############################
   +1   5.6%  ####
   +2   4.7%  ###
   +3   3.9%  ###
   +5   3.2%  ##
   +7   2.5%  ##
  +12   4.0%  ###
```

41% of moves repeat the note, 20% are a step of a tone or less, and 20% leap a fifth or more — mostly octaves. There is very little in between. A line that leaps constantly will not sound like this music, and neither will one that never repeats a note.

## Where the drums fall

From the 20 of 32 drumming tracks whose bar phase could be recovered, folded onto a sixteen-row bar. The rest are left out rather than averaged in as noise — a grid says where the rows are, not which row is beat one.

```

     1   1.3% ##
     2   3.0% ####
     3   6.1% ########
     4   9.6% ############
     5   1.5% ##
     6   5.5% #######
     7   5.0% ######
     8  11.3% ##############
     9   4.1% #####
    10   7.0% #########
    11   3.7% #####
    12   9.1% ###########

    14   4.4% #####
    15   3.5% ####
```

52% of drum hits land on a quarter-note beat (rows 0, 4, 8, 12), against the 25% they would if placement were even.

## Tempo, velocity, vibrato

- **Tempo**: 73 to 189 BPM, median 120.
- **FM velocity**: median 23 of 127, with the middle 80% between 5 and 76. Notes are not all struck at full level.
- **Vibrato**: 1255 spans. Depth median 38 cents (middle 80%: 13-90), speed median 6.0 Hz. That is well under a semitone — vibrato here colours a note, it does not bend it.
