# What Mega Drive music does, measured

From 183 transcribed tracks, 280297 notes. Every number below is counted from the corpus, not asserted.

## Channel roles

Median register and how often each channel is used at all. The roles are not a convention someone chose — they fall out of the chip: FM0 has the lowest median register of the six, and FM5 is missing from most because the DAC takes channel 6 for drums.

## How melodies move

Interval from one note to the next on the same voice, in semitones:

```
  -12   4.0%  ####
   -5   3.7%  ###
   -3   3.4%  ###
   -2   6.1%  #####
   -1   5.2%  #####
   +0  34.0%  ##############################
   +1   5.2%  #####
   +2   6.9%  ######
   +3   4.0%  ####
   +5   3.6%  ###
   +7   2.7%  ##
  +12   4.2%  ####
```

34% of moves repeat the note, 23% are a step of a tone or less, and 22% leap a fifth or more — mostly octaves. There is very little in between. A line that leaps constantly will not sound like this music, and neither will one that never repeats a note.

## Tempo, velocity, vibrato

- **Tempo**: 70 to 200 BPM, median 119.
- **FM velocity**: median 23 of 127, with the middle 80% between 5 and 64. Notes are not all struck at full level.
- **Vibrato**: 5066 spans. Depth median 18 cents (middle 80%: 10-78), speed median 6.1 Hz. That is well under a semitone — vibrato here colours a note, it does not bend it.
