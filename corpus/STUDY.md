```

   -3   2.7%  ###
   -2   6.0%  #####
   -1   6.6%  ######
   +0  32.7%  ##############################
   +1   6.7%  ######

   +3   3.6%  ###
   +5   3.8%  ###
   +7   3.0%  ###

```

33% of moves repeat the note, 26% are a step of a tone or less, and 19% leap a fifth or more — mostly octaves. There is very little in between. A line that leaps constantly will not sound like this music, and neither will one that never repeats a note.

## Tempo, velocity, vibrato

- **Tempo**: 73 to 189 BPM, median 125.
- **FM velocity**: median 21 of 127, with the middle 80% between 6 and 54. Notes are not all struck at full level.
- **Vibrato**: 1286 spans. Depth median 34 cents (middle 80%: 13-90), speed median 6.0 Hz. That is well under a semitone — vibrato here colours a note, it does not bend it.
